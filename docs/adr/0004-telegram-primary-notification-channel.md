# Telegram is the primary notification channel

Telegram is the primary external channel for time-critical notices, such as an Invite or a "who's free for lunch" prompt. A standalone web app has no push channel of its own, and Members may not read email in time for a Meetup starting in thirty minutes. Telegram was chosen over web push and chat-platform connectors because it is the channel staff already read throughout the day. A Member must link a Telegram account to receive notices there, and message content must stay minimal because it leaves the government cloud.

Members control Telegram and email delivery per notice kind. Every notice appears in the in-app inbox immediately. Enabled Telegram notices go immediately, including non-urgent kinds; enabled urgent email notices go immediately and enabled non-urgent email notices go in a daily digest.
