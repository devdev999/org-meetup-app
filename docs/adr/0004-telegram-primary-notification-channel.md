# Telegram is the primary notification channel

Time-critical notices, such as an Invite or a "who's free for lunch" prompt, are delivered first through a Telegram bot that a Member links to their profile, with in-app and email as secondary channels. A standalone web app has no push channel of its own, and email is too slow for a Meetup starting in thirty minutes. Telegram was chosen over web push and chat-platform connectors because it is the channel staff already read throughout the day. Consequences: a Member must link a Telegram account to get timely notices, and message content must stay minimal because it leaves the government cloud.

Members control Telegram and email delivery per notice kind. Every notice appears in the in-app inbox immediately. Enabled urgent notices go immediately; enabled non-urgent email notices go in a daily digest.
