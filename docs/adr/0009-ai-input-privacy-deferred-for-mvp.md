# AI input privacy restrictions are deferred for the MVP

The product owner chose to allow identifying information in AI requests for the MVP, resolving the deferred decision in [issue #25](https://github.com/devdev999/org-meetup-app/issues/25) and superseding [ADR 0007](0007-ai-requests-exclude-identifying-data.md). Canonicalisation, clustering, automatic Interest extraction and Scout may send free text and relevant context to the configured AI endpoint, including Member names, emails, Departments and Sites, without redaction, pseudonymisation or rejection because the text may identify someone. This permits those features to proceed while input privacy controls are deferred; Organisation and role access still govern the data Scout can read, Scout remains read-only, and Telegram notice content and report access rules retain their existing requirements.
